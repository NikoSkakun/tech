# plant-hydro — «ГЕС і ГАЕС: енергія води»

Diorama chapter (9 steps, child of the `plants` hub). Script: `docs/volumes/electricity/chapters/plant-hydro.md`;
physics: `@electricity/sim/plant-hydro` (M1–M7). Built by `B-plant-hydro` on the structure of `plant-wind`.

## Conventions

1. World in metres + `env.setScale(300, { adaptFrom: LOOK_SCALE.diorama })`; the reservoir level is world y
   (normal level 52 m, crest 56 m = +4 m = «перелив» of M6).
2. Cutaway rigs live on the floating stand south of the island (actor `stand.group`); one rig per step.
3. Reading and operating happen in the HUD: dock = inputs, board = charts / tables / stats, callouts for names.
4. Physics only from the sim; one model sample drives board, readouts and 3D. QA hooks: `__app.read('live' | 'we' |
'dams' | 'path' | 'turbines' | 'gov' | 'res' | 'psh' | 'ru' | 'hotspot')`, probes `hs.<id>`, `ru.pin`.
